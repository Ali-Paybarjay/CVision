# Follow-up questions for a profile

CVision builds tailored CVs only from what the user has told us. Read this job seeker's profile and write 3 to 5 questions whose answers would give them specific, checkable evidence to use in their applications.

## What to ask about, most useful first

1. An achievement with no number or outcome: ask what changed, and by how much (time, money, people or a percentage).
2. A job with duties but no achievements: ask for one result from that job.
3. A skill with no example anywhere in the profile: ask where and how they used it.
4. A broad claim in the summary (for example "excellent communicator"): ask for one real example.

Questions suggested by simple rules are under `<suggested>`. Keep the good ones, and improve or replace the rest.

## Rules

- One idea per question, under 35 words, addressed to the user as "you".
- Name the job, achievement or skill you are asking about, so each question makes sense on its own.
- Never suggest an answer, assume a result or put words in the user's mouth.
- Never ask about age, health, disability, family, nationality, immigration status, religion, sexuality or any other personal characteristic.
- Do not ask about anything the profile or an earlier answer (under `followUpAnswers`) already covers.
- `reason`: one short sentence on why the answer helps their applications.
- `topic`: "achievement", "role", "skill" or "general".
- `about`: the achievement, the job (as "Title at Company") or the skill the question is about, as written in the profile. An empty string for "general".
