Senior Process Engineer – Water and Wastewater
Halden Water Engineering
Leeds or Manchester · Hybrid · Permanent · Full-time · £55,000 to £65,000 a year

About the team
Our water team designs treatment works for water companies and industrial clients across the north of England, from first options studies to detailed design and commissioning support.

What you'll do
You will lead the process design of municipal and industrial wastewater treatment works, from optioneering to detailed design, and guide junior engineers in our Leeds and Manchester offices. The successful candidates are likely to have design experience of some of the following: fixed and suspended growth systems, anaerobic digestion, thermal hydrolysis, nutrient removal, disinfection and solids filtration. Knowledge of DSEAR and COMAH would be ideal, as several of our sites store biogas and treatment chemicals.

You will work closely with our civil, mechanical and electrical engineers, and present your designs to clients.

What you'll bring
- Process modelling with BioWin or GPS-X
- Experience chairing HAZOP sessions
- Chartered membership of a professional institution, such as IChemE or CIWEM
- Development of fee proposals, creation of P&IDs, plant layouts, GA drawings, and energy and mass balances
- Leading teams to deliver to programme and budget
- Feasibility studies and OPEX/CAPEX estimates
- Acting as the process designer in HAZOP studies

What we offer
Hybrid working, 27 days' holiday plus bank holidays, a pension with up to 8% employer contribution, and paid membership of one professional body.
